For life at sea

STCW Tracker

Track STCW certificates, expiry dates and sea service. Offline & private.

Never sign on with an expired certificate again.

STCW Tracker keeps every STCW certificate and crew document in one place on your phone, reminds you before they expire, and builds a clean PDF pack when your manning agent asks for your papers.

Your documents, ready for the next contract.

  • Store STCW certificates, offshore tickets and crew documents.
  • See what is valid, expiring soon or expired.
  • Get expiry reminders before your next contract.
  • Record sea service, including vessel, rank and sign-on and sign-off dates.
  • Create a PDF export pack to share with your manning agency or employer.
  • Lock the app with your fingerprint or device PIN.

Private by design

Works offline — useful at sea with no signal. No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking. Your records stay on your device.

Free and Pro

Free: track up to 3 certificates with reminders.

Pro: one-time purchase, no subscription. Unlimited certificates, PDF export packs and advanced reminders.

Made for deck and engine officers, ratings, cadets and offshore crew.

STCW Tracker is a personal organiser. It does not verify certificates or replace official requirements from your employer, flag state, training provider or issuing authority.
