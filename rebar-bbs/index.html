Bar schedules · Android

Rebar BBS Calculator

Turn bar dimensions into a schedule, nominal weight and a cutting plan.

Organize bar marks, quantities, cover faces and laps. Keep calculation assumptions visible as you check a component.

Schedule and units

  • BS 8666:2020 limited shapes 00, 11, 21 and 51, with cut lengths and nominal weights.
  • Metric bar sizes and ASTM #3–#11 straight bars. ASTM bends and ACI hook scheduling are unavailable.
  • Metric mm/kg or US ft-in/lb; cover and kerf in inches, stock in feet and inches in US mode.
  • Cover faces and lap allowances stay editable in the free schedule.

Free and one-time Pro

Free: single-component schedules and a local draft, with banner ads.

Optional Pro: cutting plans, whole-stock purchase weight and cost estimates, CSV and BBS + order PDF export, saved projects with multiple components, local history and no ads. No subscription. Google Play supplies the purchase price and restore service.

Stock length and kerf are editable. Prices may be per kg, metric tonne or stock bar, with an optional ISO currency code. No currency conversion is performed.

Know the assumptions

The first-fit cutting plan does not guarantee the fewest stock bars. Nominal weight is an estimate. Independently check drawings, bar specifications, cover, laps, fabrication allowances, applicable standards and available stock.

This app does not provide structural design, certify fabrication or establish regulatory compliance.

Local records and exports

Calculations work offline. No app account is required. Project inputs and results stay on the device; reports go only to a destination you choose. Advertising, consent and purchases use Google services as described in the privacy policy. Keep independent copies of important records.

Estimate for reference — verify before ordering, cutting or wiring.
