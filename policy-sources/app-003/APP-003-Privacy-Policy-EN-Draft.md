## 4. Local Storage, Security, and Deletion

Any data saved in the App’s local storage remains there until it is removed. You can remove that local app data by clearing the App’s storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the App.

The App’s local data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. The App does not provide its own cloud backup or synchronization service.

Your local app data cannot be recovered on a new device, after a device reset, or after you clear the App’s storage or uninstall the App. To keep your records, you must export them yourself and save the exported files separately before changing devices, resetting your device, clearing app storage, or uninstalling the App.

Exported files and any copies you independently store elsewhere are outside the App’s local storage. You are responsible for managing, protecting, and deleting those files and copies separately.

We do not maintain a server-side copy of your app data and cannot retrieve, restore, or delete your local data remotely. Local data is subject to your device’s access controls and security settings. Please keep your device and any copies of your records secure.
